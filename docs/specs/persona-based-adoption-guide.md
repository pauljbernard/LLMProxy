- `Proxy Only`

## Small Team

Recommended profile:

- `Standard`

Recommended path:

- `Proxy + Capture`

## Platform Team

Recommended profile:

- `Full`

Recommended path:

- `Full Learning Loop`

## Enterprise Ops Team

Recommended profile:

- `Full`

Recommended path:

- staged rollout from `Proxy Only` to `Full Learning Loop`
